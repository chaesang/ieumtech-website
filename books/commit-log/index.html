Commit Log

Where you've been is not erased.

Commit Log is a book written in Korean, published in 2026. It gathers 72 short essays in five chapters. Each one starts from a single word picked up over 30 years as an engineer: 13 years at Google, the years as a manager, a CS student in the 1990s, and the return to Korea. About the Author

Chaesang Jung is a software engineer of three decades, including thirteen years at Google, who has since led and taught engineering in Korea and the United States. He writes short essays about the ordinary words an engineer carries from one job to the next.
